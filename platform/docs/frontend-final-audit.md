## Deliberate product differences

- YouTube red and the user-selected family mark with miniature old ring/play symbol; mother vermilion remains its own brand.
- Videos/channels/live/comments and their observation units replace articles/publishers/bylines. News article totals are shown only when explicitly describing upstream vocabulary provenance.
- Maintained vocabulary, channel classifications, AI hypotheses and operator-reviewed event links remain separately labelled. They must not be silently collapsed into one concept to mimic a screenshot.
- No copied news crawling, political-camp judgments, Similarweb traffic, reading-telemetry report or unimplemented install prompt. Navigation links to implemented YouTube surfaces rather than dead mother routes.
- Additional fixture/coverage/version notes protect interpretation of local previews and legacy snapshots. They are displayed near headings/results/method explanations, not hidden in developer-only logs.
- Source avatars are locally cached and identity-bound; video thumbnails use a strict allowlisted YouTube derivative. A failed image leaves text and links usable.

The identified sidebar/header gaps are **not** included in this deliberate-difference list. Their source corrections are complete in this batch; rebuilt-image visual verification still requires root integration confirmation.
